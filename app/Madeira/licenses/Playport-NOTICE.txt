Madeira adapts the colour typeless texture usage correction from Playport.
Modified for Madeira: restrict the omission to BGRA/BGRX linear/sRGB families;
retain PixelFormatView for numeric reinterpretation, depth, BC and UAV resources.

Source: https://github.com/playportdev/playport
Reviewed revision: 412de5f (2026-10-19 review)
Original patch: patches/dxmt/0009-d3d11-keep-lossless-compression-on-colour-typeless-t.patch
Authors: The Playport authors <dev@playport.dev>

Madeira's adaptation retains PixelFormatView for depth-bound and UAV-bound
resources as well as depth/stencil and block-compressed format families. This
keeps R16/R32 typeless depth remapping and shader-write allocations on their
previous policy. Metal format views are verified separately from performance.
