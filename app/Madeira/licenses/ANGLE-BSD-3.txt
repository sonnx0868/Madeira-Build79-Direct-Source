ANGLE D3D11 OpenGL ES compatibility runtime
============================================

`arm64ec-windows/libEGL.dll` and `libGLESv2.dll` are the unmodified ANGLE
binaries from the official Electron 28.1.0 Windows x64 release:

https://github.com/electron/electron/releases/tag/v28.1.0

The binary identifies ANGLE 2.1.22152, git hash 4ae5f681dfe6. Madeira uses
only ANGLE's EGL/OpenGL ES DLLs; it does not ship the Electron runtime.

Electron archive SHA-256:
6ab17fa3ec537e7d9a5e6483f321a0634333c30a6fdfaec1cc8fd07953cbff89

libEGL.dll SHA-256:
d9c8541eaf0293c67ece10e97d00a8b689d5e043a8356d43224aac1af3a21a5f

libGLESv2.dll SHA-256:
a1275f57b47575db9aa3a577e5eacba1d7f1d5578ef6a8072468d788c93c85ff
